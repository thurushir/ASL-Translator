# LSTM + CTC on fingerspelling clips

Generated by `train_ctc.py` on 2026-11-11. 495 clips, signers: anna, saaya, thurushi.

- Every row is a **held-out signer**: the model never trained on that person's clips.
- Inputs per frame: 64 hand-shape features + hand-present flag + 14 motion features (wrist position and speed, fingertip speeds; motion.py).
- Speed augmentation: each training clip played at a random 0.7x to 1.4x speed every epoch (never the validation or test clips).
- Clips resampled to 15 fps. Model: 2-layer bidirectional LSTM (128 units) (616,219 parameters), greedy CTC decoding.
- Early stopping on a validation set taken from the two training signers only.
- *No J/Z* columns leave out words with J or Z (comparable with the baseline's columns).
- Baseline B rows are copied from `results/baseline.json` (the number to beat).
